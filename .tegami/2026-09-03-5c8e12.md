### `currentcolor` in lowercase is `currentColor`

A colour keyword matches case-insensitively now, as CSS says it does. `currentColor` was compared
exactly, so the spec's own lowercase spelling — which is what a framework generates — was not a
colour at all and the declaration holding it was thrown away. That is what made Tailwind's `ring-*`
utilities draw nothing without a `ring-<colour>` alongside them, and it took `text-current`,
`border-current`, `divide-current` and `caret-current` with it. `transparent` and `clear` match the
same way.
