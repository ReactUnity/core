### `currentcolor` in lowercase is `currentColor`

A colour keyword is matched case-insensitively now, as CSS says it is. `currentColor` was compared
exactly, so the spec's own spelling — `currentcolor`, all lowercase, which is what a framework
generates — was not a colour at all, and the declaration holding it was thrown away. That is what
made Tailwind's `ring-*` utilities draw nothing without a `ring-<colour>` alongside them, since the
ring's colour is `var(--tw-ring-color, currentcolor)`, and it took `text-current`,
`border-current`, `divide-current` and `caret-current` with it. `transparent` and `clear` are
matched the same way.
