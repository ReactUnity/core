### A percentage `rgb()` channel is 2.55 of the channel, not 255

`rgb(82%, 82%, 82%)` was read as 82 × 255 and then divided by 255 again, so the colour came out as
82 times white and rendered as plain white. That is why the two controls in the useragent stylesheet
that use percentages — a `button`'s fill and a `toggle`'s track — have been invisible against a
light page rather than the greys they declare.

Percentages are per channel, so `hsl()` and the alpha slot were never affected. This is `rgb()` and
`rgba()` only, in both the comma and the space-separated forms.
