Selectors that were silently matching nothing now match: `:only-child`, `:not()` with a list, the full `An+B`
grammar, the `-of-type` family and the attribute operators, and a `::before` no longer counts as a sibling
for the positional pseudo-classes. UIToolkit gains `:focus-within` and `:focus-visible`.
