### A Vite entry document no longer crashes the Editor, and its stylesheets apply

Pointing a scene at a Vite dev server took the Editor down with an access violation. The fault was
in QuickJS itself and is fixed in the fork ReactUnity builds; every shipped binary is rebuilt.

An entry document with more than one script used to evaluate all of them at once, which is not what
document order means. Vite's dev server inlines the React Refresh preamble and then loads the app,
and the app could look for what the preamble installs before it had been written. Host-initiated
module roots are now evaluated one at a time, in document order, on QuickJS and Jint alike, and a
root that throws no longer strands the ones queued behind it.

`<link rel="stylesheet">` and `<style>` in the entry document were dropped on the floor, which is
why a built Vite project came up completely unstyled. Both are collected in document order, so the
cascade survives, and applied at `:root` so they reach the whole tree. `preload`, `modulepreload`
and `alternate stylesheet` are skipped, matching what a browser applies.
