### An inline style lost to a rule that was entirely `!important`

When every rule matching an element was `!important`, the element's inline style was placed above
them in the cascade instead of below, so the rules it should have beaten won.

A disabled stylesheet's rules are also skipped while matching now, rather than matched with nothing
to apply.
