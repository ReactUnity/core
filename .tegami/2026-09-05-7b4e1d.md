### `:has()`

`.card:has(> img)`, `label:has(+ input:checked)`, `.list:has(.item:hover)` and the rest of the
relative selector list: descendant, child and both sibling combinators, a comma-separated list of
branches, `:is()` inside, and `:not(:has())`. It weighs what its most specific branch weighs.

Styles follow the tree. A change under or after an element a `:has()` was evaluated on re-resolves
that element and what follows it — a class, an id, a `data-*` prop, a state such as `:hover`, an
element inserted or removed. A stylesheet that does not use `:has()` re-resolves nothing extra, and
one that does never restyles the whole tree.

Spaces inside a pseudo-class argument no longer break the selector apart, whichever function it is.
