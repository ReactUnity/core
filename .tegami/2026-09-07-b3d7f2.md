### A text's background no longer covers its glyphs

On UGUI a `<text>` or `<icon>` with a `background-color`, a `background-image`, a border or a
`border-radius` drew an empty box, because UGUI paints an object before its children and the
background was the child. The glyphs now sit on a `[Text]` child kept after the background, so they
paint over it. Code that fetched the `TextMeshProUGUI` from the element's GameObject has to look in
its children now; the `Text` property on the component is unchanged.
