### A text's background no longer covers its glyphs

On UGUI a `<text>` or `<icon>` with a `background-color`, a `background-image`, a border or a
`border-radius` drew an empty box. The glyphs were a graphic on the element's own object and
the background a child of it, and UGUI paints an object before its children. The glyphs now
sit on a `[Text]` child kept after the background, so they paint over it. Code that fetched the
`TextMeshProUGUI` from the element's GameObject has to look in its children now; the `Text`
property on the component is unchanged.
