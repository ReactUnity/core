### `width: 100%` no longer collapses inside a content-sized parent

A percentage cross size resolved to zero whenever the parent's size came from its children —
[#89](https://github.com/ReactUnity/core/issues/89). A `width: 100%` divider under a column with no
width of its own simply disappeared, and the two things that brought it back, `align-self: stretch`
and a pixel width, are the two paths that lay the child out a second time.

Yoga now resolves the percentage against the size the parent's measurement produced, and eight of
the nine layouts that reproduced this are exact against Chrome. The ninth is a percentage on the
*main* axis, which is [facebook/yoga#1661](https://github.com/facebook/yoga/issues/1661) and
untouched here.

### The Yoga binaries move to current upstream

They came from a 2022 branch point and are now built from Yoga's current `main`, with everything
ReactUnity carries on top down to the one commit above.

The WebGL archive halves, from 531 KB to 256 KB, because it is no longer built through Yoga's
embind project. Android ships one `libyoga.so` per ABI in place of the single `yoga.aar`, which is
the layout `com.reactunity.quickjs` already uses. Checked on an emulator against the arm64 build,
where it lays out identically to the Windows one; not yet on real hardware.
